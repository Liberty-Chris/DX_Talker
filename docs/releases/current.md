DX Talker 0.2.0a6 adds a simple announcement format editor in Announcements.

Start with Frequency first, Callsign first or Callsign + frequency. Use Up and Down to reorder sections and uncheck any section you do not want spoken. Intro offers New DX and New DX on, or your own short text. Units stay with frequency and can be muted independently; kHz/MHz selection still controls decimal placement.

A live sentence example follows your edits. Listen to example previews the pending format using the saved voice and local output without saving unrelated settings or reconnecting the cluster. Save and apply uses the format for RF, local listening and spot previews. IDs are unchanged.

Existing installations keep their current frequency first format. At least one section is required. Source data, logs and filters retain their original kHz values. RF and output fault handling remain unchanged.

This release also includes the compact IDs page and stateful RF toggle introduced in 0.2.0a5. Future certificate alignment across macOS, Linux, iOS and Android remains planned; native trust currently applies to the Windows updater.
