DX Talker 0.2.0a25 keeps live spots updating while local listening is muted.

The desktop local control now offers Mute locally and Unmute locally. Mute cancels current and queued local audio without stopping the local session or reconnecting the cluster. New matching spots keep populating with a gray M beside the callsign. Their details explain that they were deliberately muted. They are not marked as played or rejected by a filter.

Unmute announces new spots only and does not replay the muted backlog. Existing filters, timestamp and repeat rules still apply. Zero local volume also preserves muted display rows. Explicit Connect preserves an already armed local session's mute state before starting the source. Full Stop all output, source loss, RF and audio fault behavior remain intact. Muted idle listening does not request Windows keep awake by itself.

Windows, XP and Ubuntu share this desktop change. Android retains its existing dedicated mute behavior. Saved settings and profiles remain compatible.
