DX Talker 0.2.0a23 uses US operating practice for CW inference on desktop and Android.

The earlier intersection of regional plans has been replaced. Untagged spots at 3540, 7035, 10105 and 1832 kHz now qualify as inferred CW. Lower 6m and 2m CW exclusive portions are included. Documented digital overlaps remain Unknown. Explicit reported modes still take priority and selected license, band, activity and location filters remain in force.

Help lists the complete US ranges and sources. It distinguishes preferred CW operating portions from legal permissions and explains why a Technician chart's CW only label cannot establish the mode used by another station. This update does not claim a new FCC allocation or change anyone's transmit privileges.

The desktop display/announcement receipts and unfiltered Source console labels from a22 are retained. The same classification correction is included in the Android APK. Saved filters and profiles remain intact.
