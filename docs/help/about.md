# About DX Talker

DX Talker 0.1.0a1 is the first Windows pilot of the application my dad, Dave Hockaday (WB4IUY), and Kevin Leggett (KT4BN) created in the early 1990s. I'm Chris Hockaday (KF4DBX), and I'm bringing it forward.

The original received DX cluster information through a TNC packet node, announced spots over RF and periodically identified using voice and CW. Its compiled programs and recordings are preserved in the repository. The editable source and exact QST citation are still being located.

The Windows pilot adds internet sources, configurable USB audio and serial PTT, generated speech, audio Morse and recorded voice IDs. Windows 11 x64 is the initial pilot target. Physical hardware support depends on bench verification.

Standalone WiFi hardware and direct repeater integration for ASL3, Allmon and Asterisk are planned after the Windows application.
